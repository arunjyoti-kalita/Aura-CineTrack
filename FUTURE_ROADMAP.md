

# 🚀 CineTrack — Future Roadmap

CineTrack’s roadmap focuses on deepening the strongest product experiences rather than adding unnecessary feature complexity.

The long-term goal is to evolve CineTrack into a more intelligent and emotionally immersive cinematic discovery platform.

---

# Phase 1 — Reducing Friction

Focus:
- simplify movie logging
- improve usability
- strengthen consistency

Planned improvements:

- TMDB / OMDb auto-fill integration
- smarter search suggestions
- faster onboarding experience
- improved mobile responsiveness
- richer movie metadata support

---

# Phase 2 — Personalized Discovery

Focus:
- deeper recommendation quality
- more reflective insights
- personalized cinematic exploration

Planned improvements:

- AI-assisted movie recommendations
- mood-aware discovery improvements
- deeper Taste Evolution analytics
- director progression systems
- personalized cinematic summaries

---

# Phase 3 — Community & Expansion

Focus:
- collaborative discovery
- stronger retention
- social exploration experiences

Planned improvements:

- collaborative Arena challenges
- community-driven recommendations
- shared watchlists
- cinematic recap exports
- deeper anime & series tracking

---

# Long-Term Product Vision

The long-term vision for CineTrack is to become:

> a cinematic memory and discovery platform — not just a movie tracker.

The focus will continue to prioritize:

- immersive product experiences
- emotional connection
- cinematic exploration
- low-friction journaling
- differentiated discovery systems
