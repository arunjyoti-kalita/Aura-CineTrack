

# ⚠️ CineTrack — Known Limitations

CineTrack was intentionally built as a fast-moving MVP focused on validating the core product experience before expanding feature depth.

Several areas are still intentionally lightweight or evolving.

---

# Current Limitations

| Area | Current Limitation |
|---|---|
| Movie Logging | Manual metadata entry can still feel time-consuming for new users |
| Recommendations | Discovery is currently mood-based rather than fully personalized |
| Social Layer | Community and collaborative features are intentionally limited in v1 |
| Mobile Experience | Optimized primarily for web-first usage rather than native mobile interactions |
| Analytics | Product analytics and behavioral tracking are still lightweight |
| Series Tracking | Episodic tracking exists at a basic level but lacks deeper progression systems |

---

# Why These Tradeoffs Were Accepted

The product intentionally prioritized:

- fast iteration
- low-friction UX
- differentiated core experiences
- validating product direction early
- shipping quickly with real users

Instead of trying to solve everything in v1, the focus was on strengthening the product’s most unique experiences first — particularly the World Cinema Map, Taste Evolution, and Quick Log systems.

---

# Product Reflection

One of the biggest lessons during development was realizing that adding more features does not automatically improve product quality.

In several cases, reducing complexity created better user experiences than expanding functionality.

The current roadmap focuses more on deepening the strongest experiences rather than increasing feature quantity.
